Typical Water Damage Problems in Fort Worth

Water usually escapes at the same few vulnerable spots in a home. These are the ones Fort Worth property owners report to us again and again.

Water heaters: Over the years, mineral deposits settle in a tank and corrode the metal underneath. The first sign is often a rusty puddle or a damp ring on the floor around the base. A split tank does not stop at one tankful, because the inlet keeps feeding it until the valve is shut. When the unit lives in an attic or interior closet, the water reaches carpets and ceilings far from it.

Roofs: One strong storm is enough to peel back shingles and push water beneath the roof covering. Leaks also start around chimneys, vent pipes and skylights where the sealant has dried out. Since water follows the framing downhill, the ceiling stain rarely sits directly below the actual opening. For roof leaks in Fort Worth, we tarp where needed, pull out soaked insulation and dry the structure so the ceiling can be repaired.

Freezing: Depending on the season, a hard overnight freeze can burst lines that were fine the day before. The first to go are hose bibs, irrigation lines and any pipe routed through an unheated space. A house left empty or a wing kept unheated faces the greatest risk. If a faucet only trickles on a freezing morning, shut off the water and call before the pipe thaws.

Backups: A drain that gurgles or several fixtures that slow down together point to a blockage in the main line. When the clog becomes complete, every flush and every load of laundry comes back out downstairs. The water is unsanitary, and hard surfaces need disinfecting after the solids and liquid are removed. Until a plumber opens the line, do not use sinks or toilets and keep everyone clear of the mess.

Seepage: A wet line along the bottom of a foundation wall is a sign that water is building up in the soil next to it. Short downspouts, soil that slopes toward the house and blocked footing drains are frequent causes. Early clues include powdery white residue on the concrete, flaking wall paint and air that always smells musty.
