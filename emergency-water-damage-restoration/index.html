Signs It Is Time for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • You have no way to locate or get to the shutoff while water continues to pour.
  • Pooled water is creeping over the floor into neighboring rooms or onto the staircase.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • The tank on your water heater gave out and its contents are spreading through the room.
  • Walls on the lower floor are wet from top to bottom because of a leak overhead.
  • Part of your ceiling bows downward because water is pooling on top of it.
  • Carpet, drywall or hardwood has been soaked for more than a few hours.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • A sump pump should be tried out before storms arrive, ideally with a backup battery installed.
  • Wrap plumbing in cold, unheated parts of the house so a hard freeze cannot crack it.
  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.
  • Store valuables and important papers off the floor, especially in basements and lower levels.
  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.

Why Professional Emergency Water Damage Restoration Matters

The visible water is the easy part of an emergency. What takes skill is figuring out where everything else has soaked in.
